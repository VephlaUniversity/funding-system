Our Story

4.9k+ Tech Scholarships

$16k+ Monetary Grants

200+ Secured Roles

Since its founding in November 2020, the DevTobs Funding Scheme (DTFS) has become a force for change in the African tech community. What began as a vision to remove barriers and level the playing field has grown into a transformative initiative, one that not only provides funding, but surrounds aspiring tech talents with a network of senior developers, mentors, and real-world opportunities.

Over the past three years, DTFS has funded emerging tech talent with over USD 12,000 in grants, empowering recipients to access vital resources such as laptops, devices, co-working spaces, reliable internet, mentorship programs, and vetted tech platforms. These resources have been life-changing, particularly for individuals from disadvantaged backgrounds, brilliant minds who simply needed a fair chance to thrive.

In late 2023, DTFS reached a defining milestone: several 2022 recipients, once newcomers, successfully landed tech roles fully equipped by the support they received. Armed with the right tools and guidance, these once-overlooked individuals stepped into the industry with confidence, proving that when potential meets opportunity, the results are limitless.

But the impact doesn’t stop there.

What drives DTFS is not just the success of those supported, it's the chain reaction they create. Many recipients go on to mentor, support, and uplift others, building a community rooted in collaboration, compassion, and the belief that no one should be left behind because of where they come from.

While DTFS welcomes all passionate innovators, it remains deeply committed to nurturing African tech talent, ensuring that location, financial hardship, or lack of infrastructure never define a person’s future.

DTFS isn’t just a funding scheme. It’s a movement, a growing force of equity, empowerment, and the unstoppable belief in the power of technology to transform lives.

FAQs from DTFS

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers

Who is Eligible to Apply?
Anyone who meets the specific criteria for a DTFS scholarship or grant is welcome to apply. Each program is designed to meet different needs, but in general, we prioritize individuals who show:
  • A strong passion for technology
  • Clear personal goals and motivation
  • A genuine need for financial support or mentorship Applications are open throughout the year, and special announcements may be made for limited-time opportunities.
What is the Mentorship & Transition Aid Grant?
This grant is for tech newcomers who need guidance to get started. Selected applicants receive a $150 USD grant, used to cover structured bootcamps and mentorship sessions. Each participant is paired with an experienced mentor who helps them build skills, choose a path, and prepare for real job opportunities.
What Does the Funding Scheme Cover?
Each grant or scholarship has specific coverage, but DTFS funding may include:
  • Laptops or digital devices
  • Internet or data support
  • Course and bootcamp enrollment fees
  • Certification and exam costs
  • Learning tools and resources
  • Structured mentorship and career coaching Always check the specific scholarship page for exact details.
How Do I Apply?
Visit the "Find a Scholarship" section on our website. There, you’ll be able to:
  • Explore available scholarships
  • Review each program’s criteria
  • Submit your application online Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis unless a specific deadline is mentioned.
What Is the Selection Process & Timeline?
After submitting your application, your profile will be reviewed by the DTFS team. You may receive updates within a few hours to weeks. Use the scholarship chek Portal to track your status and receive notifications.
Is the Support Fully Funded?
Most DTFS scholarships are fully funded. In some cases, when full funding is not available, we provide partial funding to cover the majority of costs. We aim to ensure that no passionate learner is left without support.
Can I apply for more than one scholarship?
Yes, you can apply for multiple scholarships as long as you meet the requirements for each. However, applicants will only be awarded one grant at a time to ensure fair access.
What if I already have a laptop?
You can still apply for other grants. Many of our programs focus on mentorship, certification, or advanced learning support and are ideal for learners who already have basic tools but need guidance or funding to move forward.
Can I reapply if I’m not selected?
Yes, absolutely. Many successful applicants were not selected on their first attempt. We encourage you to keep building your skills and reapply when ready. Stay connected to our community and follow updates for future openings.
How does DTFS select recipients?
We consider several factors:
  • Your passion for tech and what you’ve done so far
  • The clarity and realism of your goals
  • Your current challenges and resource needs
  • References or recommendations (if included) We look beyond qualifications to find people who are committed and ready to grow.
Are there any age limits?
There is no strict age limit. Most DTFS grants are open to youth, early-career professionals, or adults making career transitions. If you have the passion and potential, we welcome your application.
Do I need prior tech experience to apply?
Not always. Some programs are designed for complete beginners, while others like the Advance+ Grant are tailored for individuals with prior experience. Read each grant description carefully to find what fits your journey.
Can I get support even if I am not in school or college?
Yes. DTFS supports self-learners, career switchers, and individuals outside of traditional academic systems. If you are actively learning and have the drive to grow, you are welcome to apply.
How can I stay updated about new scholarships?
Follow us on social media, subscribe to our newsletter, and regularly check the website for announcements, openings, and updates on your application status.
I’m not sure I’m “qualified” enough. Should I still apply?
Yes, definitely. DTFS is not just about how much you already know, it's about how hungry you are to learn and grow. If you’ve been making efforts, even with limited resources, and have a passion for tech, that’s what we care about. Many of our most successful recipients started out just like you.
What if I’ve been learning on my own using free resources?
That’s exactly the kind of effort we love to see. If you’ve been studying on your phone, joining free webinars, or teaching yourself through YouTube and free courses include that in your application. It shows commitment, and we value that highly.
I don’t have a tech background. Can I still apply?
Absolutely. Many of our scholarships are designed for beginners or people transitioning into tech from other fields. Whether you're coming from education, art, finance, retail, or anywhere else if tech is your next step, we’re here to support that journey.
What if I don’t have strong English or communication skills?
Don’t let that hold you back. We’re not looking for perfect grammar or polished essays, we're looking for real stories and real passion. Just be honest, be yourself, and share your journey clearly. We understand and respect different backgrounds.
How competitive is it? Will I actually have a chance?
Each scholarship is competitive, but don’t let that discourage you. Many recipients never thought they’d be chosen but they were. If you meet the criteria and apply with sincerity, you absolutely have a chance.
What if I only need partial help can I still apply?
Yes. Whether you need full support or just a push to cross the finish line, you can apply. DTFS offers both fully funded and partially funded support based on your situation. Just explain what you’ve done so far and what help you need.
Will I have to pay anything later if I’m selected?
No hidden costs. DTFS grants and scholarships are not loans. They are gifts of support to help you grow. In some cases where full funding isn’t available, we might offer partial grants but we’ll always be transparent about what’s covered and what’s not.
Can someone help me with my application?
Yes. We sometimes offer community support, guides, and example applications to help you apply with confidence. Follow our social platforms and check our website for any ongoing support initiatives. You can also ask a mentor, teacher, or peer to review your application with you.
What makes a strong application stand out?
A great application shows:
  • Your effort so far (even if it’s on a phone or borrowed device)
  • Why you want to learn tech
  • What you plan to do with the support
  • A clear and honest story of your journey If you show passion, intention, and a willingness to grow that’s what stands out most.
