Tech Momentum grant

Accelerating Progress. Unlocking Potential.

The Tech Momentum grant also known as the Advance+ Grant is a competitive micro-grant of $350 USD, created to support experienced tech learners at pivotal moments in their journey. Designed for those who have already made significant strides in tech, this grant provides the final push needed to overcome obstacles and move confidently toward career success.

  • 🌐 Internet connectivity or upgraded access
  • 🎓 Advanced online courses or bootcamps
  • 🏢 Workspace upgrade
  • 📘 Specialized learning tools or software
  • 🏅 Certification fees to increase employability and career readiness

Who Is It For?

The Advance+ Grant is designed for applicants who:

  • Already own a laptop or primary device
  • Have completed a bootcamp and has a certification in chosen learning track
  • Can demonstrate significant self-driven learning or project work and has a portfolio
  • Face financial or logistical barriers to completing the next step (e.g. certification, advanced courses, job prep resources)
  • Are ready to transition into the workforce or a specialized tech role

Whether you’re preparing for your final certification, building a portfolio, or leveling up into a better role the Advance+ Grant is here to accelerate your journey and ensure that progress doesn’t pause for lack of support.

Because your hard work has brought you this far and we’re here to help take you further.
